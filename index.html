Questions we hear most

Are all phones factory sealed with official warranty?

Yes. Every phone is sealed, comes with a GST invoice and carries the brand's one-year warranty. We open the box with you at the counter.

Can you move my WhatsApp chats and photos?

Yes, free, in about 20 minutes while you wait. Bring your old phone and its passcode.

Who is eligible for 0% EMI?

Eligible HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank and Bajaj Finserv customers. Approval usually takes under 5 minutes at the counter with your ID.

How is my old phone's data handled?

We wipe it in front of you before it leaves your hands, and you get confirmation.

Can I reserve a phone and pick it up later?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with the model and finish, and we will hold it at your nearest store.
